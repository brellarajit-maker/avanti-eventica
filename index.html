About Us

We create experiences,
not just events.

Avanti-Eventica is a professional creative crew built around one idea — that a celebration should feel like a performance. We design choreography, direct themed showpieces and stage live music that turns a room full of guests into an audience.

From the first concept meeting to the final bow, our dancers, choreographers and musicians work as one company. And through our band Shakti-Naad, we bring devotional, classical, Bollywood and fusion music to the same stage — live, and entirely our own.

He has been a performer since 8 years of age at Doordarshan.

He has Mastered, classical, Devotional and Contemporary Music in all spheres of the world of live performance and Playback Arena.

About Dastak

Dastak ft. Parag Jain

Where versatility meets unforgettable live music

Parag Jain — singer, composer and lead vocalist of Dastak — is a versatile artist with over a decade of experience in the music industry. He began at the age of 10, and refined his voice through Hindustani Classical training under Pandit Sarathi Chatterjee of the Banaras Gharana.

Known for his soulful voice and dynamic stage presence, Parag moves between genres without dropping the room — connecting with diverse audiences across engaging, high-energy live performances.

  1. Bollywood
  2. Sufi
  3. Indian Classical (and semi-classical)
  4. Western (pop and rock)
